Bird Photographer

Plan the morning. Know the odds.

Bird Photographer is an iOS app for people who photograph birds. It answers one question: given where you are setting off from, the hours you can give it and the bird you are after, where should you go and when should you be there.

The odds it shows are fitted from 553,815,781 eBird checklist records across twenty-two metropolitan areas and 6,419 sites. They are rates over past mornings, not a forecast of today.

Data

Bird observation data are from the eBird Basic Dataset, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, Ithaca, New York. Used for research and education on a non-commercial basis. The app ships derived summaries only; no eBird records are redistributed.
